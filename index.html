Our story

We brought the whole street with us.

Madras doesn't eat in one register. There's the tiffin room at 7am, the Chettiar kitchen with its stone-ground masala, the Indo-Chinese cart behind the cinema, and the biryani shop that only really wakes up after ten.

We didn't want to pick one. So the menu on Kingsway runs the whole way across — idli and podi in the morning register, lamb chettinadu and Dindigul biryani in the middle, gobi manchurian and kothu parotta when the night gets loud. Same batter, ground here, rested overnight. Same iron, never touched with soap.

And we stay open. Past midnight on weekdays, past two on Thursday, Friday and Saturday — because that's when Madras actually gets hungry.
